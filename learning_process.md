## Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? (Explain in 1 line)

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where you explain a concept in simple words as if teaching someone else to discover and fill gaps in your understanding.

## Question 3: What are Active and Diffuse Modes of Thinking?

### Active (Focused) Mode

- Concentrate fully on a specific task or problem.
- The brain follows familiar pathways.
- Best for solving problems, studying, and practicing concepts that require close attention.

### Diffuse Mode

- A relaxed state of thinking that occurs during breaks, walking, resting, or sleeping.
- Helps the brain make broader connections between ideas.
- Useful for understanding difficult concepts and finding creative solutions.

## Question 4: According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

1. Deconstruct the skill (break it into smaller parts).
2. Learn enough to self-correct using a few good resources.
3. Remove barriers to practice by eliminating distractions.
4. Practice for at least **20 hours** to overcome the initial frustration barrier.
